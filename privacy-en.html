01 Information We Collect

a. Information you provide

ItemPurposeStored
Nickname (2–8 characters) Shown to opponents in versus mode and on leaderboards On your device and the game backend (Unity Gaming Services)

b. Information generated automatically during play

ItemDetailPurpose
Anonymous player identifier A random string issued by Unity Gaming Services, not linked to your name or email Leaderboards, saving progress, matchmaking
Gameplay records Score, play time and count, selected character and item IDs, versus results, mission and daily-reward progress, unlocked content, whether an ad was watched, purchase outcomes Saving progress, leaderboards, aggregate statistics
Device information Device model name, app version, operating system Labelling cloud backups, troubleshooting
Purchase receipts The receipt and signature issued by the app store Verifying purchases and granting items

Statistical gameplay records contain no names, email addresses, contact details, or precise location. Only numbers such as scores and times, and a fixed set of predefined field names, are transmitted.

c. Information collected by advertising SDKs

Our games are free and may include advertising. Ads are served through Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These providers may collect the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), IP address, device information, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, in accordance with their own policies.

  • How Google uses data from partner sites and apps — policies.google.com
  • Unity privacy policy — unity.com

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent dialog for personalised advertising when the game starts, and can change that choice at any time from the in-game settings.

02 What We Do Not Collect

  • Name · date of birth · gender · phone number · postal address · email address
  • Government-issued identifiers
  • Precise location (GPS)
  • Contacts · photos · call logs · messages or other personal files on the device
  • Payment card details — payments are processed by the app store and we never receive card information

Even if you sign in with a Google account, our games do not receive your email address, Google account name, or profile picture. The only thing received at sign-in is a one-time authentication code used to link the account.

03 Where Information Is Stored

WhereWhat
Your device Game progress (encrypted save file), sound and language settings, selected character and items
Unity Gaming Services Anonymous identifier, nickname, leaderboard scores, statistics, and — if you link a Google account — a backup of your progress
Google Payment processing (Google Play), ad delivery (AdMob), sign-in (Play Games Services)

Cloud backup only happens if you link a Google account. If you do not, your progress stays on the device and is lost if the app is uninstalled.

04 Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell your information. Apart from the processors listed in section 3 (advertising, analytics and backend operation), we do not share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

05 What Other Players See

When you play a match with another player, your nickname, profile picture number, best score, win/draw/loss record, and in-match score are shown on their screen. This information is sent to their device only for the duration of the match.

If you appear on a leaderboard, your nickname and score are visible to other players.

06 Retention and Deletion

  • Information stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Information stored on the backend is retained until you request deletion.
  • Statistical information is retained in a non-identifying form to improve the service.

07 Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your information by any of the following means:

  • In game — sign out or change your nickname from Settings.
  • Uninstall — removes all information stored on the device.
  • Backend deletion — email us at the address below, including your in-game nickname so we can identify the anonymous record.
  • Opt out of personalised ads — on Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID.

Users in the EEA and the UK have rights under the GDPR, including data portability and the right to object to processing. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, the right to delete, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

08 Children’s Privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in the Republic of Korea), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have collected such information, we delete it promptly. Parents and guardians who believe their child’s information has been collected may contact us below.

09 Security

  • Game progress stored on the device is protected with AES-256 encryption and HMAC integrity verification.
  • Cloud backups are transmitted and stored in encrypted form, so the backend operator cannot read their contents.
  • All communication with the backend is encrypted using HTTPS.

10 Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the change and its effective date on this page. Material changes will also be announced in the game.
