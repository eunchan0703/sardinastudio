Privacy Policy

Space Smasher · Sardina Studio

Sardina Studio (“we”, “the Studio”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the game Space Smasher (“the Game”) collects, why, and how it is handled.

Summary — The Game has no account registration and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or postal address. The nickname used in versus mode is a display name you choose yourself and does not need to be your real name. The Game is playable without signing in.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information you provide

ItemPurposeStored
Nickname (2–8 characters) Shown to your opponent in 1v1 versus mode and on leaderboards On your device and on the game backend (Unity Gaming Services)

b. Information generated automatically during play

ItemDetailPurpose
Anonymous player identifier A random string issued by Unity Gaming Services. It is not linked to your name or email address Leaderboards, saving progress, versus matchmaking
Gameplay records Score, play time and count, selected character and bullet IDs, versus results, mission and daily-reward progress, unlocked planets, whether an ad was watched, purchase outcomes Saving progress, leaderboards, aggregate statistics to improve the game
Device information Device model name, app version, operating system Labelling cloud backups (“which device is this save from”) and troubleshooting
Purchase receipts The receipt and signature issued by Google Play Verifying purchases and granting items (fraud prevention)
Statistical gameplay records contain no names, email addresses, contact details, or precise location. Only numbers such as scores and times, and a fixed set of predefined field names, are transmitted.

c. Information collected by advertising SDKs

The Game is free and includes advertising. Ads are served through Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These providers may collect the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), IP address, device information, and ad interaction data in order to deliver and measure ads, in accordance with their own policies.

  • How Google uses data from partner sites and apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Unity privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent dialog for personalised advertising when the Game starts, and can change that choice at any time from the in-game settings.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Name, date of birth, gender, phone number, postal address, email address
  • Government-issued identifiers
  • Precise location (GPS)
  • Contacts, photos, call logs, messages, or other personal files on the device
  • Payment card details — payments are processed by Google Play and the Studio never receives card information

Even if you sign in with a Google account, the Game does not receive your email address, Google account name, or profile picture. The only thing the Game receives at sign-in is a one-time authentication code used to link the account.

3. Where Information Is Stored

WhereWhat
Your device Game progress (encrypted save file), sound and language settings, selected character and bullet, tutorial completion
Unity Gaming Services (game backend) Anonymous identifier, nickname, leaderboard scores, statistics, and — if you link a Google account — a backup of your game progress
Google Payment processing (Google Play), ad delivery (AdMob), sign-in (Play Games Services)

Cloud backup only happens if you link a Google account. If you do not, your progress stays on the device and is lost if the app is uninstalled.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell your information. Apart from the processors listed in section 3 (advertising, analytics and backend operation), we do not share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

5. What Your Opponent Sees in Versus Mode

When you play a 1v1 match, your nickname, profile picture number, best score, win/draw/loss record, and in-match score are shown on your opponent’s screen. This information is sent to the opponent’s device only for the duration of the match.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Information stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Information stored on the backend is retained until you request deletion.
  • Statistical information is retained in a non-identifying form to improve the service.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your information by any of the following means:

  • In game — sign out or change your nickname from Settings.
  • Uninstall — removes all information stored on the device.
  • Backend deletion — email us at the address below. Please include your in-game nickname so we can identify the anonymous record.
  • Opt out of personalised ads — on Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID.

Users in the EEA and the UK have rights under the GDPR, including data portability and the right to object to processing. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, the right to delete, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in the Republic of Korea), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have collected such information, we delete it promptly. Parents and guardians who believe their child’s information has been collected may contact us at the address below.

9. Security

  • Game progress stored on the device is protected with AES-256 encryption and HMAC integrity verification.
  • Cloud backups are transmitted and stored in encrypted form, so the backend operator cannot read their contents.
  • All communication with the backend is encrypted using HTTPS.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the change and its effective date on this page. Material changes will also be announced in the Game.
