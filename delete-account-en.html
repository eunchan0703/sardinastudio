Delete your account

We will delete your account and saved data for any Sardina Studio game. Just send us an email.

01 How to request

Email sardina.chans@gmail.com. Including the two items below helps us act faster.

  • Game name — for example, Space Smasher
  • The Google account you signed in with — we use it to find your saved data.

To verify it is really you, please write from the same Google account you signed in with. If it differs we may ask one follow-up question.

02 What gets deleted

DeletedKept
Account identifier, game data backed up on our servers (scores, currency, progress), nickname, leaderboard entries, mailbox Purchase receipts — Korean law requires us to keep these for 5 years. They are not linked back to your game progress

Anything stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. This request covers the server side.

03 After you send it

We process the request once we have confirmed it, and reply when it is done. Deletion cannot be undone.
