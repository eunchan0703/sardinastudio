Studio

Sardina Studio is a one-person game studio. We make games that fit into the gaps of a day — three minutes waiting for a bus, ten minutes before sleep.

We prefer things that feel good in the hand over things that are big and complicated. Games whose rules fit in one sentence, but take a while to get good at.
