Cross Word Puzzles — WORDRIA

Swipe the letters, fill the board.

Swipe across the scattered letters to spell a word and the empty squares fill in, one row at a time. That is the whole rule — and yet finishing one board makes you want the next. Plenty of stages, special stages and bonuses, all free, and it keeps going with no internet at all.
