FAQ

Bugs & errors

The game freezes or behaves oddly.

Sending these along helps us find the cause much faster:

  • Game name
  • Device model and Android version
  • App version
  • When it happened and what you were doing
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one

Email a bug report

Where do I find the app version?

In Android Settings › Apps, pick the game — the version is shown near the bottom. Menu names can differ slightly between devices.

Payments & refunds

I paid but did not receive my item.

Email us the order number from your Google Play receipt, the game name, and the date and time of purchase.

Email about a payment

I would like a refund.

Payments are processed by Google Play; the studio never receives card details. You can request a refund by following Google Play Help's “Request a refund on Google Play” guide.

Google asks you to contact the developer for items bought more than 48 hours ago. In that case, email us with your order number.

Data recovery

I changed phones or reinstalled, and my progress is gone.

Cloud backup only happens if you linked a Google account. Without it, progress is kept on the device only and is removed when the app is uninstalled (Privacy Policy, section 3).

If you did link a Google account and still cannot see your progress, email us with the game name and what happened.

Email about recovery

Account & privacy

I want to delete my account and data.

How to ask, and what is deleted or kept, is set out on the Delete your account page.

I do not want personalised ads.

On Android, go to Settings › Privacy › Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID. See Privacy Policy, section 7 for details.

What information do the games handle?

It is set out in the Privacy Policy, which covers every game by the studio. What each game handles is listed in the Data safety section of its Google Play listing.
